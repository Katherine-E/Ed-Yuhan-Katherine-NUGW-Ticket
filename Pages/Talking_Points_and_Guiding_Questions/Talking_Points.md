Below are my core values, main talking points and guiding questions to help you get to know more about me and my views on key issues. 

----
## What are your core values?

Integrity, Accountability, Balance, and Communication

I want to be accountable for my job and make sure that I am doing my best even when no one is watching. I also know that without balancing workload among each other and ensuring good communication we can’t be good organizers and can't represent the workers whom we are stewards for. 

----
## What is your vision for the next year?

Next year will involve three big focuses as CCS, they are not completely separate and build upon each other. 

**First, we must recruit and elect new stewards ahead of our bargaining fight.** This means reaching workers who may be interested but also communicating with them about how to be a steward and why, especially this year, having stewards in as many departments as possible is more important than ever. This starts to lay the organizing framework we will need during our contract fight and even if we don't find stewards for every department we must make contacts in every department. **I also envision working heavily with the new DCSs and existing stewards to accomplish this as they will know their divisions more intimately than I do and will be our most effective tools in reaching workers and new stewards.**

**Secondly we must be organized for and during our contract fight.** I envision that this will entail utilizing those contacts we made while recruiting stewards to hear what our members care about. **Our bargaining will need to be as transparent as possible to membership because that is what they deserve. I see CCS as the information conduit for this transparency.** I must work with DCSs to make sure as stewards we are speaking directly to workers, that no concern is being overlooked and no one feels silenced or underrepresented. I must also make sure this information is getting to the BC through myself and the DCSs. **Having membership input will never be useful unless we are listening to it and having bargaining information be available isn't useful unless we are making sure its accessible to our workers.**

**Thirdly, we must continue our campaigns of grievances whether it be for unit exclusions, discipline and discharge, or any other issue our workers are facing. Not only are grievances an opportunity to protect and empower workers but they are critical evidence at the bargaining table** and prove to administration that we will not give up on fighting for our workers just because bargaining is around the corner. **Then after we have our new contract we must make it as easy to understand for members as possible, this includes holding town halls, training, communications, info graphics and flyers. Workers deserve to know their rights under the contract so that they can know when to file a grievance if that contract is violated.** I envision this will involve much collaboration between myself, the grievance committee and stewards so we can continue to use our organizing skills to protect workers under ___their___ contract.

## Tell us about yourself. How did you get started in NUGW? / What motivates you to stay?

I got started in NUGW because a local steward in my department saw that I cared about the details of how workers were treated. They got me involved as a steward and encouraged me to step up as McCormick DCS when the opportunity came. **I stayed because of the impact I saw that I was having as a steward and as DCS. I saw that we were helping  our colleagues, we were representing the issues they cared about, making sure they weren’t overlooked by the union or by Northwestern. That is what motivates me to stay, knowing that the work we do as stewards and as a union is genuinely helping our workers.**

----
## Why do you want to run for this role?

**I want to be Campus Chief Steward because I believe I have the skills and motivation to use this role to continue helping workers across our union. I know first hand how important it is to listen to our workers, to empower them and their voices, and to support them in organizing around key issues.** I have also seen how issues that may seem isolated to a specific division like discipline and discharge are important across the entire university and **I want to enable our union to make those connections so we can utilize experiences in one division to help workers in another. I also know how hard the job of our executive board and bargaining committee is and how hard it can be being a local steward. I believe I can foster a supportive community that enables our stewards to be the best organizers they can be.** I want to be CCS because I think I could do a good job and am willing to listen to constructive input from those around me to build a better union and work environment at Northwestern for us all.

## Why do you think you're the best for the role and what experience do you have to help you do well in this role?

**I believe I am the best choice for campus chief steward because I have a track record of organizing around the issues our workers care about most. I have helped organize what will become the discipline and discharge working group after seeing how prevalent cases have become where administration utilizes loop holes to exclude workers from the bargaining unit and ultimately from their programs.** This organizing, its related petitions and grievances that I have been involved with in McCormick protect all workers, especially international students, activists for Palestinian liberation, those who refuse the biased “anti-bias training", first year student struggling to get placed in labs, workers on leave who need to retain their healthcare, workers facing unfair practices surrounding qualifying exams or prospectus, the list goes on and on. **In addition to this organizing I have been part of a push alongside my current co-DCS Pallavi to file list exclusion grievances every quarter and have won several grievances to get Presidential and terminal year fellows in-unit. Pallavi and I have also successfully gotten 100% of in-unit workers to sign a member or agency card in winter, spring and summer 2026, and I single handedly made connections to underrepresented departments to achieve this effort.** It is all of these successful and sustained efforts of organizing that I have done as a DCS that make me the best candidate to be CCS. **Our workers who deserve a CCS will continue to fight for what matters to them and who will not rest until every single worker is protected, and who will work with every DCS and steward to make sure our organizing is successful, and I believe I can be that CCS.**
