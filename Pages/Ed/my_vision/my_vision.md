# President's Vision

## A leader is only as good as their ability to trust in the people around them
We are a union of three thousand people, and the work done by our union is through all of us. I have an unwavering trust that the members of NUGW collectively want to do the work that makes us so powerful and have the ability to complete it. It will be my job as president to ensure this work gets done through delegation and coordination. After all, many hands make light work!

## Empower members to recognize organizing as a solution
For people to be willing to do the work, they first need to have the hope that there is a solution and that the solution can be achieved through organizing. I believe that I can inspire hope in others, whether that is through my willingness to learn or my tenacity in a fight. As president, it will be my duty to turn this hope into a strong, unified force that the university cannot possibly overcome. 

## Build spaces that promote creative, bottom-up organizing from members
I believe that, as our main organizing body, we should always have concrete tasks and discussions at Contract Action Team (CAT) meetings and GMMs. In the past, these tasks have been walkthroughs, phonebanking, and art builds. While these are great tasks, I believe we should expand CAT to include even more meaningful action items, such as how we can organize a town hall over specific bargaining proposals or drafting messaging around our key campaigns in this contract fight. I will strive to make CAT a space where members can weigh in on contract language and bargaining strategy directly.

## Follow the lead of our working groups
I believe that our Working Groups — international workers (DIAS), healthcare and accessibility, trans rights, and Title IX — are our strongest organizing bodies as we currently stand. Within those groups, we have learned more about how we can fight for the protections that we deserve. I will have consistent meetings and check-ins with these groups to ensure that our vision for our union and contract fight aligns with those who have done the extra labor on specific causes.

## Be a leader that personally understands the stakes of the contract and of a strike
Being able to practice astronomy is a dream that I never believed that I could achieve. I am ecstatic to work on my current research as a PhD worker and, if all goes well, I hope to continue my research in academia. I believe a majority of our members have a similar passion for their research, so this contract fight and our collective organizing is and will continue to be as personal for me as it is for you. My dedication to our organizing stems from these personal stakes: I understand the fears that members may have in a strike and I understand how these wins would transform our lives. I hope to be a president that embodies my passion for both my research work and our union as we build this contract fight together.
