# Candidate Statement

I’m running for Campus Chief Steward because I am passionate about creating a better and safer work environment for all of us! I believe we are a stronger union when every worker is represented, has a voice and is empowered to organize around issues they care about most. As CCS, my priorities will be to recruit stewards especially to our underserved divisions, to connect with and energize as many workers as possible to enable transparent, powerful, and accessible bargaining, and to promote understanding of workers rights under our new contract so we can continue our ongoing campaign of grievances. As a DCS I have learned and used the skills I need to make these priorities a reality. I helped organize around discipline and discharge, pursue list-exclusion grievances,connected with underrepresented departments, and  built membership participation across my division with 100% of in-unit cards signed each quarter. As CCS I will fight for transparent and member-driven bargaining. I must listen to the diverse needs of all workers, build a culture of open communication, and support member-lead organizing efforts. I will fight to make sure no member is left behind. 

For more information about me and my campaign please visit [my campaign website](https://katherine-e.github.io/Katherine-Ellis-Campaign-For-Chief-Campus-Steward/) and feel free to contact me via email at [katell@u.northwestern.edu](katell@u.northwestern.edu)
